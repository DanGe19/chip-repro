Locomotion vs. interaction by hours: paper ≈ 77 / 23 %, ours ≈ 79 / 21 %. The paper filters TRUMANS and OMOMO into subsets without stating the rule. Ours: OMOMO — all clips of the four objects used in the paper; TRUMANS — contiguous segments with no object-interaction action label and the person standing/walking (pelvis-to-foot height > 0.7 m), ≥ 3 s, subsampled to 8.71 h. The two small components (standalone locomotion, box transport) are not yet included.
